Privacy Policy

1. Information in your browser

The browser's localStorage holds your current life, up to 30 completed lives, starred moments, settings, notes, parting words and feedback drafts. A four-digit classroom code may be included in a life record. The Japanese and English editions use separate storage keys.

2. Network and feedback

The site does not automatically upload life records. If you choose to share, copy or export, the selected content goes to a device sharing app, clipboard or saved file. Loading pages still involves ordinary web requests, and the host may receive normal access information. The site currently has no analytics, ads, public API, support inbox, AI email triage or issue-record system.

You may save feedback drafts locally. If you choose to open a draft in your email app, its text and page, language and device details are handed to that app. You choose the recipient and whether to send. Check the draft before adding personal information or life records.

3. Deletion and exports

“Delete all” under “Past lives” removes this site's records, settings and drafts from both language editions. You can also clear this site's data in your browser. Private browsing or clearing storage may remove records permanently. Keep exported files under your own control.

4. Classroom use

Regular play is intended for ages 15 and up; classroom mode is intended for ages 12 and up. Classroom mode stores the current life locally but does not add completed lives to the regular Past lives archive. Avoid student names and school identifiers in notes or classroom codes. Clear records on shared devices after class and follow your school's data policy.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page.
