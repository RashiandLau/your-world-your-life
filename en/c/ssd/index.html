If you were born in South Sudan

About 0.27% of babies born worldwide arrive in South Sudan. That is 1 in 370: about 356,181 each year, or 0.7 each minute.

9.7% of babies born in South Sudan die before age five. That is about 48 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 60.7 years for women and 54.8 years for men.
