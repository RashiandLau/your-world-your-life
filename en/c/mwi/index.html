If you were born in Malawi

About 0.52% of babies born worldwide arrive in Malawi. That is 1 in 192: about 691,877 each year, or 1.3 each minute.

4.9% of babies born in Malawi die before age five. That is about 24 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 70.8 years for women and 64.3 years for men.
