If you were born in Ethiopia

About 3.18% of babies born worldwide arrive in Ethiopia. That is 1 in 31: about 4,263,170 each year, or 8.1 each minute.

4.5% of babies born in Ethiopia die before age five. That is about 22 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 71.0 years for women and 64.4 years for men.
