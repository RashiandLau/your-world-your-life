If you were born in Cameroon

About 0.74% of babies born worldwide arrive in Cameroon. That is 1 in 135: about 996,297 each year, or 1.9 each minute.

6.5% of babies born in Cameroon die before age five. That is about 32 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 66.2 years for women and 61.8 years for men.

    Source: UN World Population Prospects 2024 (via Our World in Data)

    Society

    The former British Southern Cameroons joined to form a federal republic.

    Source: Major historical events
  4. 1972
    Society

    Source: Major historical events
