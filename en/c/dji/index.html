If you were born in Djibouti

About 0.02% of babies born worldwide arrive in Djibouti. That is 1 in 5,000: about 24,784 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

4.9% of babies born in Djibouti die before age five. That is about 24 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 68.7 years for women and 63.7 years for men.
