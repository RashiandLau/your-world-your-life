If you were born in Paraguay

About 0.10% of babies born worldwide arrive in Paraguay. That is 1 in 1,000: about 138,354 each year, or 0.3 each minute.

1.6% of babies born in Paraguay die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 77.1 years for women and 71.0 years for men.
