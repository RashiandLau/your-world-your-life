If you were born in Kazakhstan

About 0.30% of babies born worldwide arrive in Kazakhstan. That is 1 in 333: about 406,891 each year, or 0.8 each minute.

0.9% of babies born in Kazakhstan die before age five. That is about 4 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 78.5 years for women and 70.3 years for men.
