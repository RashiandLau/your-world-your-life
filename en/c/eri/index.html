If you were born in Eritrea

About 0.08% of babies born worldwide arrive in Eritrea. That is 1 in 1,250: about 103,590 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

3.4% of babies born in Eritrea die before age five. That is about 17 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 70.9 years for women and 66.8 years for men.
