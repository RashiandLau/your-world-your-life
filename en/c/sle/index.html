If you were born in Sierra Leone

About 0.20% of babies born worldwide arrive in Sierra Leone. That is 1 in 500: about 265,546 each year, or 0.5 each minute.

9.1% of babies born in Sierra Leone die before age five. That is about 45 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 63.7 years for women and 60.2 years for men.
