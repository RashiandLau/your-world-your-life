If you were born in Cuba

About 0.07% of babies born worldwide arrive in Cuba. That is 1 in 1,429: about 94,279 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

0.9% of babies born in Cuba die before age five. That is about 4 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 80.7 years for women and 75.9 years for men.
