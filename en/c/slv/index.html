If you were born in El Salvador

About 0.07% of babies born worldwide arrive in El Salvador. That is 1 in 1,429: about 99,283 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

1.0% of babies born in El Salvador die before age five. That is about 5 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 76.5 years for women and 67.8 years for men.
