If you were born in South Africa

About 0.89% of babies born worldwide arrive in South Africa. That is 1 in 112: about 1,196,530 each year, or 2.3 each minute.

3.5% of babies born in South Africa die before age five. That is about 18 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 69.8 years for women and 62.8 years for men.
