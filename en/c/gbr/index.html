If you were born in United Kingdom

About 0.51% of babies born worldwide arrive in United Kingdom. That is 1 in 196: about 687,365 each year, or 1.3 each minute.

0.5% of babies born in United Kingdom die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 83.3 years for women and 79.5 years for men.
