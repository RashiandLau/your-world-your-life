If you were born in Norway

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in Norway. That is 1 in 2,500: about 54,425 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.2% of babies born in Norway die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 84.8 years for women and 81.6 years for men.
