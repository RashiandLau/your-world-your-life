If you were born in Timor-Leste

About 0.02% of babies born worldwide arrive in Timor-Leste. That is 1 in 5,000: about 30,878 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

4.8% of babies born in Timor-Leste die before age five. That is about 24 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 69.7 years for women and 66.3 years for men.
