If you were born in Netherlands

About 0.12% of babies born worldwide arrive in Netherlands. That is 1 in 833: about 166,406 each year, or 0.3 each minute.

0.4% of babies born in Netherlands die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 83.4 years for women and 80.6 years for men.
