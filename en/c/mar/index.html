If you were born in Morocco

About 0.47% of babies born worldwide arrive in Morocco. That is 1 in 213: about 630,341 each year, or 1.2 each minute.

1.6% of babies born in Morocco die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 77.8 years for women and 73.3 years for men.
