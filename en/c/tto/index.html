If you were born in Trinidad and Tobago

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Trinidad and Tobago. That is 1 in 10,000: about 14,420 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

1.9% of babies born in Trinidad and Tobago die before age five. That is about 9 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 76.8 years for women and 70.5 years for men.
