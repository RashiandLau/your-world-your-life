If you were born in Burundi

About 0.36% of babies born worldwide arrive in Burundi. That is 1 in 278: about 477,906 each year, or 0.9 each minute.

4.7% of babies born in Burundi die before age five. That is about 24 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 65.9 years for women and 61.8 years for men.
