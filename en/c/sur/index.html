If you were born in Suriname

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Suriname. That is 1 in 10,000: about 10,975 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

1.6% of babies born in Suriname die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 77.0 years for women and 70.6 years for men.
