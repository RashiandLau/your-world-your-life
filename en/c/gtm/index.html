If you were born in Guatemala

About 0.29% of babies born worldwide arrive in Guatemala. That is 1 in 345: about 385,325 each year, or 0.7 each minute.

2.0% of babies born in Guatemala die before age five. That is about 10 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 75.0 years for women and 70.4 years for men.
