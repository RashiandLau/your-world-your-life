If you were born in Lebanon

About 0.07% of babies born worldwide arrive in Lebanon. That is 1 in 1,429: about 93,345 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

1.8% of babies born in Lebanon die before age five. That is about 9 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.9 years for women and 75.9 years for men.
