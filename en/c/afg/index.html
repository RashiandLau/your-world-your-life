If you were born in Afghanistan

About 1.14% of babies born worldwide arrive in Afghanistan. That is 1 in 88: about 1,534,851 each year, or 2.9 each minute.

5.3% of babies born in Afghanistan die before age five. That is about 26 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 67.8 years for women and 64.7 years for men.
