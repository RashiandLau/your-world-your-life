If you were born in Kyrgyzstan

About 0.11% of babies born worldwide arrive in Kyrgyzstan. That is 1 in 909: about 142,455 each year, or 0.3 each minute.

1.7% of babies born in Kyrgyzstan die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 76.6 years for women and 68.4 years for men.
