If you were born in Panama

About 0.05% of babies born worldwide arrive in Panama. That is 1 in 2,000: about 72,531 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

1.5% of babies born in Panama die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 82.7 years for women and 76.9 years for men.
