If you were born in Pakistan

About 5.21% of babies born worldwide arrive in Pakistan. That is 1 in 19: about 6,996,078 each year, or 13.3 each minute.

5.6% of babies born in Pakistan die before age five. That is about 28 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 70.3 years for women and 65.5 years for men.
