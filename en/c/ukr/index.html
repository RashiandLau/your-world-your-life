If you were born in Ukraine

About 0.16% of babies born worldwide arrive in Ukraine. That is 1 in 625: about 213,067 each year, or 0.4 each minute.

0.8% of babies born in Ukraine die before age five. That is about 4 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.4 years for women and 69.8 years for men.
