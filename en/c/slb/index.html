If you were born in Solomon Islands

About 0.02% of babies born worldwide arrive in Solomon Islands. That is 1 in 5,000: about 22,398 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

2.0% of babies born in Solomon Islands die before age five. That is about 10 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 72.2 years for women and 69.4 years for men.
