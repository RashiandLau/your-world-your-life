If you were born in Ghana

About 0.68% of babies born worldwide arrive in Ghana. That is 1 in 147: about 908,866 each year, or 1.7 each minute.

3.6% of babies born in Ghana die before age five. That is about 18 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 68.2 years for women and 63.3 years for men.
