If you were born in Equatorial Guinea

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in Equatorial Guinea. That is 1 in 2,500: about 58,141 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

6.8% of babies born in Equatorial Guinea die before age five. That is about 34 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 65.9 years for women and 62.2 years for men.
