If you were born in Namibia

About 0.06% of babies born worldwide arrive in Namibia. That is 1 in 1,667: about 80,076 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

4.0% of babies born in Namibia die before age five. That is about 20 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 71.5 years for women and 63.5 years for men.
