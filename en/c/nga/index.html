If you were born in Nigeria

About 5.75% of babies born worldwide arrive in Nigeria. That is 1 in 17: about 7,717,990 each year, or 14.7 each minute.

11.6% of babies born in Nigeria die before age five. That is about 58 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 54.9 years for women and 54.3 years for men.
