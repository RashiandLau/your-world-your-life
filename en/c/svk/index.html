If you were born in Slovakia

About 0.03% of babies born worldwide arrive in Slovakia. That is 1 in 3,333: about 46,017 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.6% of babies born in Slovakia die before age five. That is about 3 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 81.7 years for women and 75.2 years for men.
