If you were born in Brunei

About under 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Brunei. That is fewer than 1 in 20,000: about 6,215 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

1.0% of babies born in Brunei die before age five. That is about 5 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 77.7 years for women and 73.5 years for men.
