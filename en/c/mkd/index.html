If you were born in North Macedonia

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in North Macedonia. That is 1 in 10,000: about 16,024 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.3% of babies born in North Macedonia die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 78.9 years for women and 74.5 years for men.
