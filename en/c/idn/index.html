If you were born in Indonesia

About 3.36% of babies born worldwide arrive in Indonesia. That is 1 in 30: about 4,503,538 each year, or 8.6 each minute.

1.8% of babies born in Indonesia die before age five. That is about 9 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 73.4 years for women and 69.2 years for men.
