If you were born in Egypt

About 1.84% of babies born worldwide arrive in Egypt. That is 1 in 54: about 2,475,625 each year, or 4.7 each minute.

2.2% of babies born in Egypt die before age five. That is about 11 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 74.0 years for women and 69.7 years for men.
