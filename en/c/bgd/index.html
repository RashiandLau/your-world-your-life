If you were born in Bangladesh

About 2.62% of babies born worldwide arrive in Bangladesh. That is 1 in 38: about 3,511,630 each year, or 6.7 each minute.

3.0% of babies born in Bangladesh die before age five. That is about 15 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 76.7 years for women and 73.3 years for men.
