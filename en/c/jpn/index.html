If you were born in Japan

About 0.52% of babies born worldwide arrive in Japan. That is 1 in 192: about 703,190 each year, or 1.3 each minute.

0.2% of babies born in Japan die before age five. Life expectancy is 87.1 years for women and 81.1 years for men.
