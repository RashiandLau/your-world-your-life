If you were born in Zambia

About 0.53% of babies born worldwide arrive in Zambia. That is 1 in 189: about 717,110 each year, or 1.4 each minute.

4.8% of babies born in Zambia die before age five. That is about 24 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 68.9 years for women and 64.1 years for men.
