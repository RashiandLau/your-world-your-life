If you were born in Honduras

About 0.18% of babies born worldwide arrive in Honduras. That is 1 in 556: about 238,266 each year, or 0.5 each minute.

1.5% of babies born in Honduras die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 75.7 years for women and 70.5 years for men.
