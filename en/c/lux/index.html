If you were born in Luxembourg

About under 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Luxembourg. That is fewer than 1 in 20,000: about 6,526 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.2% of babies born in Luxembourg die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 85.5 years for women and 81.0 years for men.
