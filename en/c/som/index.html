If you were born in Somalia

About 0.62% of babies born worldwide arrive in Somalia. That is 1 in 161: about 832,300 each year, or 1.6 each minute.

10.1% of babies born in Somalia die before age five. That is about 50 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 61.6 years for women and 56.5 years for men.
