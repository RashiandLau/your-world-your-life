If you were born in Cambodia

About 0.27% of babies born worldwide arrive in Cambodia. That is 1 in 370: about 362,475 each year, or 0.7 each minute.

1.8% of babies born in Cambodia die before age five. That is about 9 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 73.4 years for women and 68.1 years for men.
