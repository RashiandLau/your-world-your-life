If you were born in Spain

About 0.24% of babies born worldwide arrive in Spain. That is 1 in 417: about 320,808 each year, or 0.6 each minute.

0.3% of babies born in Spain die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 86.5 years for women and 81.4 years for men.
