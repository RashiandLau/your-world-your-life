If you were born in Türkiye

About 0.70% of babies born worldwide arrive in Türkiye. That is 1 in 143: about 944,664 each year, or 1.8 each minute.

1.0% of babies born in Türkiye die before age five. That is about 5 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 80.3 years for women and 74.6 years for men.
