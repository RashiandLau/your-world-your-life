If you were born in Libya

About 0.09% of babies born worldwide arrive in Libya. That is 1 in 1,111: about 123,029 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

1.0% of babies born in Libya die before age five. That is about 5 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 72.7 years for women and 69.6 years for men.
