If you were born in Madagascar

About 0.77% of babies born worldwide arrive in Madagascar. That is 1 in 130: about 1,038,338 each year, or 2.0 each minute.

6.2% of babies born in Madagascar die before age five. That is about 31 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 65.6 years for women and 62.1 years for men.
