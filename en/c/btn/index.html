If you were born in Bhutan

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Bhutan. That is 1 in 10,000: about 9,978 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

1.7% of babies born in Bhutan die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 75.3 years for women and 71.6 years for men.
