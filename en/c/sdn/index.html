If you were born in Sudan

About 1.27% of babies born worldwide arrive in Sudan. That is 1 in 79: about 1,704,644 each year, or 3.2 each minute.

6.2% of babies born in Sudan die before age five. That is about 31 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 69.8 years for women and 63.4 years for men.
