If you were born in Tunisia

About 0.12% of babies born worldwide arrive in Tunisia. That is 1 in 833: about 165,100 each year, or 0.3 each minute.

1.2% of babies born in Tunisia die before age five. That is about 6 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.3 years for women and 74.1 years for men.
