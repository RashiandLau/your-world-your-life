If you were born in Maldives

About under 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Maldives. That is fewer than 1 in 20,000: about 5,608 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.5% of babies born in Maldives die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 83.0 years for women and 79.9 years for men.
