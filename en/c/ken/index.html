If you were born in Kenya

About 1.16% of babies born worldwide arrive in Kenya. That is 1 in 86: about 1,551,536 each year, or 3.0 each minute.

3.9% of babies born in Kenya die before age five. That is about 20 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 66.1 years for women and 61.6 years for men.
