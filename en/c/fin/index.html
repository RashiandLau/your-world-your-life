If you were born in Finland

About 0.03% of babies born worldwide arrive in Finland. That is 1 in 3,333: about 44,042 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.2% of babies born in Finland die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 85.0 years for women and 79.8 years for men.
