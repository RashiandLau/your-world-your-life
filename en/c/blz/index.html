If you were born in Belize

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Belize. That is 1 in 10,000: about 7,634 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

1.3% of babies born in Belize die before age five. That is about 6 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 76.7 years for women and 71.1 years for men.
