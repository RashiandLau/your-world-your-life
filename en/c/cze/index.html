If you were born in Czechia

About 0.06% of babies born worldwide arrive in Czechia. That is 1 in 1,667: about 83,829 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

0.3% of babies born in Czechia die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 83.0 years for women and 77.1 years for men.
