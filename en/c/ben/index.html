If you were born in Benin

About 0.37% of babies born worldwide arrive in Benin. That is 1 in 270: about 495,084 each year, or 0.9 each minute.

7.5% of babies born in Benin die before age five. That is about 38 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 62.4 years for women and 59.5 years for men.
