If you were born in Switzerland

About 0.06% of babies born worldwide arrive in Switzerland. That is 1 in 1,667: about 79,104 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

0.4% of babies born in Switzerland die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 86.2 years for women and 82.7 years for men.
