If you were born in United Arab Emirates

About 0.08% of babies born worldwide arrive in United Arab Emirates. That is 1 in 1,250: about 113,048 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

0.5% of babies born in United Arab Emirates die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 84.3 years for women and 82.2 years for men.
