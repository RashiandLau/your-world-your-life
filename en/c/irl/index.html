If you were born in Ireland

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in Ireland. That is 1 in 2,500: about 54,844 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.4% of babies born in Ireland die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 84.7 years for women and 81.4 years for men.
