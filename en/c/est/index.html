If you were born in Estonia

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Estonia. That is 1 in 10,000: about 9,702 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.2% of babies born in Estonia die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 83.7 years for women and 75.1 years for men.
