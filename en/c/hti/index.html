If you were born in Haiti

About 0.19% of babies born worldwide arrive in Haiti. That is 1 in 526: about 261,267 each year, or 0.5 each minute.

5.3% of babies born in Haiti die before age five. That is about 26 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 68.5 years for women and 61.9 years for men.
