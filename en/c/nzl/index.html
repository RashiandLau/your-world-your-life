If you were born in New Zealand

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in New Zealand. That is 1 in 2,500: about 58,731 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.5% of babies born in New Zealand die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 83.7 years for women and 80.4 years for men.
