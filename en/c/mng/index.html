If you were born in Mongolia

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in Mongolia. That is 1 in 2,500: about 60,316 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

1.3% of babies born in Mongolia die before age five. That is about 6 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 77.1 years for women and 67.9 years for men.
