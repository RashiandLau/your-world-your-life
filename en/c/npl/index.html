If you were born in Nepal

About 0.42% of babies born worldwide arrive in Nepal. That is 1 in 238: about 561,619 each year, or 1.1 each minute.

2.5% of babies born in Nepal die before age five. That is about 12 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 72.1 years for women and 69.1 years for men.
