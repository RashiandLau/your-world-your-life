If you were born in South Korea

About 0.18% of babies born worldwide arrive in South Korea. That is 1 in 556: about 242,917 each year, or 0.5 each minute.

0.3% of babies born in South Korea die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 86.6 years for women and 80.8 years for men.
