If you were born in Philippines

About 1.38% of babies born worldwide arrive in Philippines. That is 1 in 72: about 1,856,212 each year, or 3.5 each minute.

2.6% of babies born in Philippines die before age five. That is about 13 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 73.0 years for women and 67.0 years for men.
