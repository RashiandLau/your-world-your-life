If you were born in Myanmar

About 0.67% of babies born worldwide arrive in Myanmar. That is 1 in 149: about 903,061 each year, or 1.7 each minute.

3.7% of babies born in Myanmar die before age five. That is about 18 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 70.4 years for women and 64.0 years for men.
