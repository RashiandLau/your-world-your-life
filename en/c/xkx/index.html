If you were born in Kosovo

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Kosovo. That is 1 in 10,000: about 18,689 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.9% of babies born in Kosovo die before age five. That is about 4 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 80.3 years for women and 76.0 years for men.
