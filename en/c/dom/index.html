If you were born in Dominican Republic

About 0.15% of babies born worldwide arrive in Dominican Republic. That is 1 in 667: about 202,991 each year, or 0.4 each minute.

3.1% of babies born in Dominican Republic die before age five. That is about 16 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 77.1 years for women and 70.7 years for men.
