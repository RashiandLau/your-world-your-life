If you were born in Eswatini

About 0.02% of babies born worldwide arrive in Eswatini. That is 1 in 5,000: about 29,706 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

4.5% of babies born in Eswatini die before age five. That is about 22 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 67.1 years for women and 61.3 years for men.
