If you were born in Costa Rica

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in Costa Rica. That is 1 in 2,500: about 51,483 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

1.0% of babies born in Costa Rica die before age five. That is about 5 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 83.6 years for women and 78.4 years for men.
