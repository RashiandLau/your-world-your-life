If you were born in Angola

About 1.08% of babies born worldwide arrive in Angola. That is 1 in 93: about 1,450,650 each year, or 2.8 each minute.

4.9% of babies born in Angola die before age five. That is about 24 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 67.3 years for women and 62.3 years for men.
