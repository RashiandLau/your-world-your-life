If you were born in Belarus

About 0.05% of babies born worldwide arrive in Belarus. That is 1 in 2,000: about 63,884 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.2% of babies born in Belarus die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.2 years for women and 69.7 years for men.
