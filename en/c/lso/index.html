If you were born in Lesotho

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in Lesotho. That is 1 in 2,500: about 56,157 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

6.0% of babies born in Lesotho die before age five. That is about 30 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 60.4 years for women and 55.0 years for men.
