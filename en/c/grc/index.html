If you were born in Greece

About 0.05% of babies born worldwide arrive in Greece. That is 1 in 2,000: about 68,732 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.4% of babies born in Greece die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 84.4 years for women and 79.4 years for men.
