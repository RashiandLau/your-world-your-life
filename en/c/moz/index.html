If you were born in Mozambique

About 0.98% of babies born worldwide arrive in Mozambique. That is 1 in 102: about 1,319,369 each year, or 2.5 each minute.

5.9% of babies born in Mozambique die before age five. That is about 30 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 66.7 years for women and 60.5 years for men.
