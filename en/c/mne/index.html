If you were born in Montenegro

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Montenegro. That is 1 in 10,000: about 6,979 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.2% of babies born in Montenegro die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 80.4 years for women and 75.5 years for men.
