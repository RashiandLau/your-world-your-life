If you were born in Ecuador

About 0.20% of babies born worldwide arrive in Ecuador. That is 1 in 500: about 271,056 each year, or 0.5 each minute.

1.3% of babies born in Ecuador die before age five. That is about 6 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 80.3 years for women and 74.9 years for men.
