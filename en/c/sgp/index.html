If you were born in Singapore

About 0.03% of babies born worldwide arrive in Singapore. That is 1 in 3,333: about 45,223 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.3% of babies born in Singapore die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 85.6 years for women and 81.2 years for men.
