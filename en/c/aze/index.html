If you were born in Azerbaijan

About 0.08% of babies born worldwide arrive in Azerbaijan. That is 1 in 1,250: about 102,470 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

1.8% of babies born in Azerbaijan die before age five. That is about 9 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 77.3 years for women and 71.7 years for men.
