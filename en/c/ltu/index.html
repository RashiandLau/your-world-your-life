If you were born in Lithuania

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Lithuania. That is 1 in 10,000: about 19,066 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.3% of babies born in Lithuania die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 81.6 years for women and 73.0 years for men.
