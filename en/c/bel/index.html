If you were born in Belgium

About 0.08% of babies born worldwide arrive in Belgium. That is 1 in 1,250: about 109,864 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

0.4% of babies born in Belgium die before age five. That is about 2 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 84.4 years for women and 80.3 years for men.
