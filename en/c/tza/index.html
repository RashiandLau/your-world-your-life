If you were born in Tanzania

About 1.83% of babies born worldwide arrive in Tanzania. That is 1 in 55: about 2,455,067 each year, or 4.7 each minute.

3.7% of babies born in Tanzania die before age five. That is about 18 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 70.0 years for women and 64.4 years for men.
