If you were born in Guinea

About 0.37% of babies born worldwide arrive in Guinea. That is 1 in 270: about 503,198 each year, or 1.0 each minute.

9.2% of babies born in Guinea die before age five. That is about 46 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 62.1 years for women and 59.7 years for men.
