If you were born in Bahrain

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Bahrain. That is 1 in 10,000: about 19,257 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

0.9% of babies born in Bahrain die before age five. That is about 4 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 82.1 years for women and 80.9 years for men.
