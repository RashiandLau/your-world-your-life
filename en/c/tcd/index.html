If you were born in Chad

About 0.68% of babies born worldwide arrive in Chad. That is 1 in 147: about 908,704 each year, or 1.7 each minute.

9.7% of babies born in Chad die before age five. That is about 48 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 57.2 years for women and 53.4 years for men.
