If you were born in Iran

About 0.87% of babies born worldwide arrive in Iran. That is 1 in 115: about 1,161,783 each year, or 2.2 each minute.

1.1% of babies born in Iran die before age five. That is about 6 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.8 years for women and 76.0 years for men.
