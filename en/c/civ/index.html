If you were born in Côte d'Ivoire

About 0.77% of babies born worldwide arrive in Côte d'Ivoire. That is 1 in 130: about 1,032,507 each year, or 2.0 each minute.

6.5% of babies born in Côte d'Ivoire die before age five. That is about 32 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 64.3 years for women and 60.2 years for men.
