If you were born in Republic of the Congo

About 0.15% of babies born worldwide arrive in Republic of the Congo. That is 1 in 667: about 197,283 each year, or 0.4 each minute.

3.9% of babies born in Republic of the Congo die before age five. That is about 20 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 67.7 years for women and 64.3 years for men.
