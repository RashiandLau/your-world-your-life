If you were born in Guyana

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Guyana. That is 1 in 10,000: about 16,622 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

2.5% of babies born in Guyana die before age five. That is about 12 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 74.1 years for women and 66.6 years for men.
