If you were born in Bulgaria

About 0.04% of babies born worldwide arrive in Bulgaria. That is 1 in 2,500: about 53,396 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.6% of babies born in Bulgaria die before age five. That is about 3 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.5 years for women and 72.2 years for men.
