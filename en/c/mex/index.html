If you were born in Mexico

About 1.52% of babies born worldwide arrive in Mexico. That is 1 in 66: about 2,040,427 each year, or 3.9 each minute.

1.3% of babies born in Mexico die before age five. That is about 6 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 78.0 years for women and 72.4 years for men.
