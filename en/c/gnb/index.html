If you were born in Guinea-Bissau

About 0.05% of babies born worldwide arrive in Guinea-Bissau. That is 1 in 2,000: about 66,496 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

6.7% of babies born in Guinea-Bissau die before age five. That is about 34 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 66.5 years for women and 61.8 years for men.
