If you were born in Austria

About 0.06% of babies born worldwide arrive in Austria. That is 1 in 1,667: about 77,349 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

0.3% of babies born in Austria die before age five. That is about 1 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 84.3 years for women and 79.8 years for men.
