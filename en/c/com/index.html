If you were born in Comoros

About 0.02% of babies born worldwide arrive in Comoros. That is 1 in 5,000: about 24,925 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

3.9% of babies born in Comoros die before age five. That is about 20 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 69.2 years for women and 65.0 years for men.
