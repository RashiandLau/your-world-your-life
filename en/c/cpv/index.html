If you were born in Cabo Verde

About under 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Cabo Verde. That is fewer than 1 in 20,000: about 6,445 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

1.1% of babies born in Cabo Verde die before age five. That is about 6 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.4 years for women and 73.0 years for men.
