If you were born in Rwanda

About 0.30% of babies born worldwide arrive in Rwanda. That is 1 in 333: about 408,845 each year, or 0.8 each minute.

3.8% of babies born in Rwanda die before age five. That is about 19 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 70.1 years for women and 65.7 years for men.
