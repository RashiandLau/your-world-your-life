If you were born in Fiji

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Fiji. That is 1 in 10,000: about 16,670 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

2.9% of babies born in Fiji die before age five. That is about 14 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 69.5 years for women and 65.5 years for men.
