If you were born in Vietnam

About 1.02% of babies born worldwide arrive in Vietnam. That is 1 in 98: about 1,363,757 each year, or 2.6 each minute.

1.7% of babies born in Vietnam die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 79.4 years for women and 70.1 years for men.
