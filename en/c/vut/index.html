If you were born in Vanuatu

About 0.01% of babies born worldwide arrive in Vanuatu. That is 1 in 10,000: about 9,268 each year, or 0.0 each minute.

1.7% of babies born in Vanuatu die before age five. That is about 8 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 74.1 years for women and 69.6 years for men.
