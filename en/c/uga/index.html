If you were born in Uganda

About 1.32% of babies born worldwide arrive in Uganda. That is 1 in 76: about 1,769,490 each year, or 3.4 each minute.

4.9% of babies born in Uganda die before age five. That is about 24 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 71.4 years for women and 65.5 years for men.
