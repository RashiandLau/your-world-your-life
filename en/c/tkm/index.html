If you were born in Turkmenistan

About 0.12% of babies born worldwide arrive in Turkmenistan. That is 1 in 833: about 159,280 each year, or 0.3 each minute.

3.9% of babies born in Turkmenistan die before age five. That is about 20 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 73.0 years for women and 67.0 years for men.
