If you were born in Oman

About 0.07% of babies born worldwide arrive in Oman. That is 1 in 1,429: about 92,190 each year, or 0.2 each minute.

1.0% of babies born in Oman die before age five. That is about 5 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 82.0 years for women and 78.7 years for men.
