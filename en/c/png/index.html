If you were born in Papua New Guinea

About 0.19% of babies born worldwide arrive in Papua New Guinea. That is 1 in 526: about 261,440 each year, or 0.5 each minute.

3.9% of babies born in Papua New Guinea die before age five. That is about 20 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 69.2 years for women and 63.8 years for men.
