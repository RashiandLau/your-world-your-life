If you were born in Senegal

About 0.41% of babies born worldwide arrive in Senegal. That is 1 in 244: about 552,510 each year, or 1.1 each minute.

3.6% of babies born in Senegal die before age five. That is about 18 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 71.0 years for women and 67.0 years for men.
