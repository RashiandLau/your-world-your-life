If you were born in Botswana

About 0.05% of babies born worldwide arrive in Botswana. That is 1 in 2,000: about 62,416 each year, or 0.1 each minute.

3.3% of babies born in Botswana die before age five. That is about 16 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 71.9 years for women and 66.8 years for men.
