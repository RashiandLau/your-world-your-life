If you were born in Tajikistan

About 0.20% of babies born worldwide arrive in Tajikistan. That is 1 in 500: about 273,260 each year, or 0.5 each minute.

2.8% of babies born in Tajikistan die before age five. That is about 14 times Japan's 0.2%. Life expectancy is 74.1 years for women and 69.7 years for men.
