Help with your records

Answers about saving, moving and deleting your records.

  • I closed the page partway through
  • I want to move records to another device or browser
  • My records disappeared after clearing browser data
  • How can I see a previous life again?
  • How can I delete everything?
  • I cannot hear the music, or want to stop it
  • What if I still need help?

I closed the page partway through

Open the first screen in the same browser and choose “Continue” to pick up from the saved age.

I want to move records to another device or browser

Choose “Move records” at the bottom of the first screen, then “Export” to save a file. On the destination browser, open the same panel and choose “Import”. Only completed lives can be moved; finish an active life before exporting it.

My records disappeared after clearing browser data

Records exist only in the browser. Clearing its data removes them, and private browsing may discard them when the window closes. Export any lives you want to keep.

How can I see a previous life again?

Open “Past lives” on the first screen. Up to 30 completed lives are kept. Select a row to read its final record.

How can I delete everything?

Choose “Delete all” in “Past lives” to remove all records and settings in this browser. This cannot be undone.

I cannot hear the music, or want to stop it

Music plays only after you press the ♪ button. Press it again to stop. Check your device's sound settings too.

What if I still need help?

This educational sample does not provide individual support. You may also read the About page.
