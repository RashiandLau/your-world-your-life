Terms of Use

Related: Privacy Policy · Legal notice

1. About the site

Your World, Your Life is an educational life simulation based on public statistics. Read About for more detail.

2. Price

Everything is free. There are no paid features or products.

3. Accuracy

Statistics are approximate. The chance of events occurring is modeled for this site. Do not use the simulation to judge a particular country or person.

4. Records

Records are saved only in your browser and cannot be recovered after deletion. See the Privacy Policy.

5. Changes and availability

The site's content may change, or the site may stop being available, without advance notice.

Figures draw on the World Bank's World Development Indicators (CC BY 4.0), including WHO/UNICEF DTP3 and measles immunization estimates, the World Happiness Report via Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0), WHO Global Health Estimates and GHO indicators, UNDP expected schooling via Our World in Data, UNAIDS HIV prevalence via Our World in Data, UCDP conflict data via Our World in Data, and UNHCR refugee data. New life-course and governance measures also use World Bank Gender Statistics, ASPIRE, and Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI, CC BY 4.0). Financial and occupational measures also use World Bank Global Findex and ILOSTAT (both CC BY 4.0). Some homicide rates use WHO mortality estimates. Missing values are estimated from comparable countries and marked as estimates. Figures are rounded for this educational site.
