How this site is made

After following a life, you may wonder about the work behind the scenes. You can also start here before playing.

If you are returning, think of a life you followed before. As you read, consider what might change in another life and what might remain beyond anyone's control.

Why follow one life?

A national average cannot show how one person's time feels. Here, a draw selects a birthplace and household. A life then moves through school, work, relationships and later years. You make choices, but you cannot choose every outcome.

The aim is to leave room for more than one story within the same conditions. Brief scenes in the record are an invitation to ask what a number can describe, and what it cannot.

From sources to a story

We read public figures on births, death by age and expected schooling, then match them to place and year. When observations are missing, we estimate from comparable conditions and label the result. An observation from another year is not treated as a fact about this person's year.

Life expectancy is not a promised lifespan. Under-five mortality does not mean every child faces identical risks. We check the source figures separately from the probabilities created to move a simulated life forward.

Connecting choices and memories

A pile of unrelated events does not feel like one life. We keep track of how an earlier decision affects later days, where family members live and what they know. A small memory can return years later.

We avoid spectacle around hardship. Words and text colour carry the tone. We also limit the lines shown in a year so a meaningful choice or event stays easy to find without more controls or screens.

Checking and revising

Fixed seeds let us replay lives and look for impossible school or work paths and household balances that do not add up. Larger samples reveal shifts in lifespan, early childhood mortality, debt, schooling and how often wishes are fulfilled.

Neither this reading nor the simulation is a complete portrait of the world. We keep revising it while showing where sources end and approximations begin. Your records stay on your device, and you decide whether to share anything outside it.
